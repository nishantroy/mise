## Beta App Description

Mise is a personal recipe app that helps you cook more easily. Save recipes from any website (or paste your own), and Mise turns them into a clean recipe you can actually cook from:

- A prep list first — what to start early, what to chop, what to mix ahead — so you're ready before the heat goes on.
- Cook mode: one step at a time in big text, with the ingredients and amounts for that step, and tap-to-start timers that ring even on silent.
- "I'm out of…" while cooking: pick the missing ingredient and get swaps from what you have.
- Tell it what you changed ("used 2 tbsp oil", "cooked the chicken 10 min on high") and it updates the recipe.
- "What can I cook?": ask in your own words ("something spicy with chicken under 30 min") and it searches your recipes against your pantry.
- A cooking log with a photo each time you make something.

Everything runs on your iPhone. On iPhone 15 Pro and newer with Apple Intelligence turned on, Mise uses Apple's on-device model for smarter suggestions; on other iPhones everything still works with built-in rules.

## What to Test

Thanks for trying Mise! Please:

1. Save a few recipes you actually cook: in Safari, tap Share → Mise, or tap + in the app and paste a link.
2. Cook one with "Start cooking": try the prep list, the step timers, and "Missing an ingredient?".
3. Afterwards, tap "I made this" (add a photo!) or "Change recipe" and describe what you did differently in your own words.
4. Try "What can I cook?" at the top of Recipes.

Tell me anything that's confusing, wrong, or slow — especially recipes that import badly (send the link). The quickest way to send feedback: take a screenshot while using Mise and tap "Share Beta Feedback", or open the TestFlight app → Mise → Send Beta Feedback.
