**Pepper Chicken (serves 4)**

**Pepper masala**
- 1¾ tbsp black peppercorns
- 1 tsp cumin seeds

**Chicken**
- 1 lb (450 g) bone-in chicken (or boneless thighs)
- 2½ tbsp oil (coconut oil preferred)
- ½ tsp cumin seeds (for tempering, optional)
- 2 medium onions (~270 g), thinly sliced
- 2–3 green chillies, slit
- 1 sprig curry leaves
- 1¾ tbsp ginger garlic paste
- Scant 1 tsp turmeric powder
- 1¾ tbsp coriander powder
- 1¾ tsp garam masala
- Salt to taste
- 1 tsp lemon juice
- Cilantro to garnish

**Instructions**
1. Toast: Dry pan, medium-low heat. Toast peppercorns and cumin 1–2 min until fragrant. Don't let them smoke.
2. Grind: Cool completely, then grind coarse. Set aside.
3. Temper: Heat oil in a kadai. Add cumin seeds and let them sizzle (or skip).
4. Onions: Add onions, curry leaves, green chillies. Sauté 5–10 min until light golden.
5. Aromatics: Add ginger garlic paste. Sauté 1–2 min until the raw smell goes.
6. Spices: Lower heat. Add turmeric, coriander powder, garam masala, salt. Stir 20–30 sec.
7. Chicken: Add chicken and coat well. Cover and cook on low 25–35 min until tender (boneless: ~15–20 min). No water needed.
8. Dry out: Uncover, raise heat, cook until the masala clings to the chicken.
9. Finish: Toss in the pepper masala (keep a pinch back to adjust), then lemon juice and cilantro.
10. Serve: With rice, roti, or naan.
